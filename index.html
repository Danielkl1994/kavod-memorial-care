Our Mission

Peace of Mind, No Matter the Distance

Living in the US means you can’t visit the cemetery in Israel as often as you’d like. Over time, sun, weather, and dust wear headstones down and leave them looking neglected. We act as your hands and heart in Israel — we visit the gravesite, clean and restore the monument, and send you high-quality photos so you can feel close, wherever you are.

  1. 01

    We Visit & Assess

    We go to the cemetery, locate the grave, and document the headstone’s current condition with photos.

  2. 02

    We Clean & Restore

    Gentle, stone-safe cleaning, weeding, repairs, and lettering touch-up — carried out with dignity and care.

  3. 03

    You Receive a Report

    A complete before & after photo report sent straight to your phone or email, so you see every detail.

Our Services

Cleaning · Preservation · Repair

Every stone is different. We use methods suited to granite, marble, and Jerusalem stone, so the monument is protected — never damaged — by our work.

Deep Cleaning

Removal of dust, dirt, moss, stains, and bird droppings, plus thorough weeding around the grave.

Crack & Stone Repair

Professional repair of cracks, chips, and loose parts to restore the monument’s stability and appearance.

Lettering & Preservation

Touch-up of faded Hebrew lettering and protective treatment to slow weathering for years to come.

Ongoing Care

Scheduled visits before the Yahrzeit or every month, with fresh flowers, candles, a small stone placed on the headstone in keeping with Jewish custom, and photo updates.

Accessibility Statement

Kavod Memorial Care is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We designed it to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and Israeli Standard IS 5568.

What we have done:

  • The site can be navigated fully by keyboard, with a “Skip to main content” link and visible focus indicators.
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA color-contrast requirements.
  • All form fields have clear labels, and errors are announced to screen readers.
  • The layout adapts to any screen size and supports browser text zoom up to 200%.
  • Animations are turned off for visitors who enable “reduce motion” on their device.

Known limitations:

Our contact form sends your request through WhatsApp, a third-party service whose accessibility we do not control. If this is difficult for you, please call us instead.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have difficulty using any part of this site, please contact Danielle at +972 58-576-0255 (phone or WhatsApp) or by email at daniellesnailer@gmail.com. We will do our best to respond within a few business days and to provide the information you need in another way.
